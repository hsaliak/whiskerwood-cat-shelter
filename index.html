A gentle first day

There is no clock. Care for your cats and meet today’s visitor at your own pace.

  1. Select a cat to see its personality and needs. Lower need numbers are better; cats speak up when a need rises.
  2. Food, water, treats, and rest help with needs. Play and feather toys use energy; kind care builds bond.
  3. Every other day, choose whether to take part in a small shelter event or pass.
  4. Read the visitor’s care clues, then accept or turn them away. Correct choices earn 1 point; wrong choices lose 1.
  5. A completed adoption earns 2 points. Choose End day only when you are ready to advance time.
